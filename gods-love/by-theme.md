# By Theme

For the days when the plan isn't what you need. Find the line that matches where
you actually are, and read there instead.

World English Bible (public domain). **Yahweh** = **the LORD** in most Bibles.

---

## When you feel forgotten

> Can a woman forget her nursing child, that she should not have compassion on
> the son of her womb? Yes, these may forget, yet I will not forget you!
> Behold, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands.
> — **Isaiah 49:15–16**

> Don't be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by your name. You
> are mine.
> — **Isaiah 43:1**

> Aren't five sparrows sold for two assaria coins? Not one of them is forgotten
> by God. But the very hairs of your head are all counted. Therefore don't be
> afraid. You are of more value than many sparrows.
> — **Luke 12:6–7**

Also: Psalm 139:1–18 · Isaiah 40:27–31

---

## When you feel guilty, or you've just failed

> But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners,
> Christ died for us.
> — **Romans 5:8**

> As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions
> from us.
> — **Psalm 103:12**

> Who is a God like you, who pardons iniquity, and passes over the disobedience
> of the remnant of his heritage? He doesn't retain his anger forever, because
> he delights in loving kindness.
> — **Micah 7:18**

> There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus.
> — **Romans 8:1**

Also: 1 John 1:9 · Psalm 130:3–4 · Luke 15:20–24

---

## When you're afraid

> Don't you be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be dismayed, for I am your God.
> I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the
> right hand of my righteousness.
> — **Isaiah 41:10**

> There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear.
> — **1 John 4:18**

> Don't be afraid, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give
> you the Kingdom.
> — **Luke 12:32**

Also: Psalm 27:1 · Psalm 23 · Romans 8:15

---

## When you feel far off, or like you've drifted

> I will heal their waywardness. I will love them freely; for my anger is turned
> away from them.
> — **Hosea 14:4**

> But while he was still far off, his father saw him, and was moved with
> compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him.
> — **Luke 15:20**

> Yes, I have loved you with an everlasting love. Therefore I have drawn you
> with loving kindness.
> — **Jeremiah 31:3**

Also: Isaiah 55:6–7 · Joel 2:12–13 · James 4:8

---

## When you're worn out

> Even to old age I am he, and even to gray hairs I will carry you. I have made,
> and I will bear. Yes, I will carry, and will deliver.
> — **Isaiah 46:4**

> Like a father has compassion on his children, so Yahweh has compassion on
> those who fear him. For he knows how we are made. He remembers that we are
> dust.
> — **Psalm 103:13–14**

> It is because of Yahweh's loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because
> his compassion doesn't fail. They are new every morning.
> — **Lamentations 3:22–23**

Also: Matthew 11:28–30 · Isaiah 40:29–31 · Psalm 62:5–8

---

## When you doubt he actually wants you

> He will rejoice over you with joy. He will calm you in his love. He will
> rejoice over you with singing.
> — **Zephaniah 3:17**

> You shall no more be termed "Forsaken" … but you shall be called "My delight
> is in her" … for Yahweh delights in you.
> — **Isaiah 62:4**

> Behold, how great a love the Father has given to us, that we should be called
> children of God!
> — **1 John 3:1**

Also: Jeremiah 32:41 · Ephesians 1:4–6 · John 16:27

---

## When you're afraid of losing something, or someone

> Because your loving kindness is better than life, my lips shall praise you.
> — **Psalm 63:3**

> For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed, but my loving kindness
> will not depart from you, and my covenant of peace will not be removed.
> — **Isaiah 54:10**

> For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life … nor things present, nor
> things to come … will be able to separate us from God's love which is in
> Christ Jesus our Lord.
> — **Romans 8:38–39**

Also: Psalm 73:25–26 · 2 Corinthians 4:16–18

---

## When you're grateful and want words for it

> Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures
> forever.
> — **Psalm 136:1**

> Your loving kindness, Yahweh, is in the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to
> the skies.
> — **Psalm 36:5**

> …to him who loves us, and washed us from our sins by his blood — and he made
> us to be a Kingdom, priests to his God and Father — to him be the glory and
> the dominion forever and ever.
> — **Revelation 1:5–6**

Also: Psalm 103:1–5 · Psalm 117 · Ephesians 1:3–8
