# The Love of God — Thirty Days

One passage a day. Read it twice: once to hear it, once slowly. Then take the
line at the bottom with you.

Texts are from the World English Bible (public domain). Where you see
**Yahweh**, most Bibles print **the LORD**.

---

## Day 1 — It was never earned

> Yahweh didn't set his love on you nor choose you, because you were more in
> number than any people; for you were the fewest of all peoples; but because
> Yahweh loves you, and because he desires to keep the oath which he swore to
> your fathers.
>
> — Deuteronomy 7:7–8

*The reason he loves you is in him, not in you. That is why it holds.*

---

## Day 2 — What he says his name means

> Yahweh! Yahweh, a merciful and gracious God, slow to anger, and abundant in
> loving kindness and truth, keeping loving kindness for thousands, forgiving
> iniquity and disobedience and sin.
>
> — Exodus 34:6–7

*Asked to describe himself, this is what he led with.*

---

## Day 3 — Everlasting

> Yahweh appeared of old to me, saying, "Yes, I have loved you with an
> everlasting love. Therefore I have drawn you with loving kindness."
>
> — Jeremiah 31:3

*It had no beginning. It will have no end. You arrived in the middle of it.*

---

## Day 4 — Called by name

> But now Yahweh who created you, Jacob, and he who formed you, Israel, says:
> "Don't be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by your name.
> You are mine."
>
> — Isaiah 43:1

*Not the crowd. Your name.*

---

## Day 5 — Precious in his sight

> Since you have been precious and honored in my sight, and I have loved you,
> therefore I will give people in your place, and nations instead of your life.
>
> — Isaiah 43:4

*He puts a price on you out loud, and it is a scandalous one.*

---

## Day 6 — He cannot forget you

> "Can a woman forget her nursing child, that she should not have compassion on
> the son of her womb? Yes, these may forget, yet I will not forget you!
> Behold, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands."
>
> — Isaiah 49:15–16

*Where he would have to look to lose track of you, your name is already cut.*

---

## Day 7 — Steadier than the mountains

> "For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed, but my loving
> kindness will not depart from you, and my covenant of peace will not be
> removed," says Yahweh who has mercy on you.
>
> — Isaiah 54:10

*Name the most permanent thing you know. His love outlasts it.*

---

## Day 8 — He carries you to the end

> Even to old age I am he, and even to gray hairs I will carry you. I have made,
> and I will bear. Yes, I will carry, and will deliver.
>
> — Isaiah 46:4

*The one who made you has agreed to carry you the whole way.*

---

## Day 9 — He sings

> Yahweh, your God, is among you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice
> over you with joy. He will calm you in his love. He will rejoice over you with
> singing.
>
> — Zephaniah 3:17

*He is not merely willing to have you. He is glad about you.*

---

## Day 10 — New every morning

> It is because of Yahweh's loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because
> his compassion doesn't fail. They are new every morning. Great is your
> faithfulness.
>
> — Lamentations 3:22–23

*Yesterday spent nothing. There is a fresh supply waiting when you wake.*

---

## Day 11 — Drawn with kindness

> I drew them with cords of a man, with ties of love; and I was to them like
> those who lift up the yoke on their necks; and I bent down to him and I fed
> him.
>
> — Hosea 11:4

*He stoops. That is the posture he takes toward you.*

---

## Day 12 — He delights in mercy

> Who is a God like you, who pardons iniquity, and passes over the disobedience
> of the remnant of his heritage? He doesn't retain his anger forever, because
> he delights in loving kindness.
>
> — Micah 7:18

*Mercy is not something he does reluctantly. It is what he enjoys.*

---

## Day 13 — As high as the heavens

> Yahweh is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loving
> kindness. … For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his
> loving kindness toward those who fear him. As far as the east is from the
> west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us.
>
> — Psalm 103:8, 11–12

*Two immeasurable distances: the size of his love, and the distance he has put
between you and your sin.*

---

## Day 14 — Like a father

> Like a father has compassion on his children, so Yahweh has compassion on
> those who fear him. For he knows how we are made. He remembers that we are
> dust.
>
> — Psalm 103:13–14

*He is not disappointed to discover you are weak. He knew. He made you.*

---

## Day 15 — Wide enough to hide in

> Your loving kindness, Yahweh, is in the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to
> the skies. … How precious is your loving kindness, God! The children of men
> take refuge under the shadow of your wings.
>
> — Psalm 36:5, 7

*It is not only vast. It is a place you are allowed to go.*

---

## Day 16 — Better than life

> Because your loving kindness is better than life, my lips shall praise you.
>
> — Psalm 63:3

*Everything you are afraid of losing is worth less than what you already have.*

---

## Day 17 — It surrounds

> Many sorrows come to the wicked, but loving kindness shall surround him who
> trusts in Yahweh.
>
> — Psalm 32:10

*Not a reward at the end. A perimeter, now, on every side.*

---

## Day 18 — He goes on being good

> For Yahweh is good. His loving kindness endures forever; his faithfulness to
> all generations.
>
> — Psalm 100:5

*Long after you, still true. Long before you, already true.*

---

## Day 19 — "I have loved you"

> "I have loved you," says Yahweh.
>
> — Malachi 1:2

*The shortest sentence in the collection. Say it back slowly until you hear it.*

---

## Day 20 — Given, not lent

> For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever
> believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.
>
> — John 3:16

*The most familiar verse you know. Read it as if it just arrived.*

---

## Day 21 — Loved while unlovely

> But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners,
> Christ died for us.
>
> — Romans 5:8

*He did not wait for you to improve. He moved first, at your worst.*

---

## Day 22 — Nothing can separate

> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Could oppression, or anguish,
> or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword? … For I am
> persuaded that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor
> things present, nor things to come, nor powers, nor height, nor depth, nor any
> other created thing will be able to separate us from God's love which is in
> Christ Jesus our Lord.
>
> — Romans 8:35, 38–39

*Go through the list. Put your own fear on it. It still doesn't make the cut.*

---

## Day 23 — Loved while dead

> But God, being rich in mercy, for his great love with which he loved us, even
> when we were dead through our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ.
>
> — Ephesians 2:4–5

*You contributed nothing. The dead don't. That is the point.*

---

## Day 24 — Beyond knowing

> …that you may be strengthened to comprehend with all the saints what is the
> width and length and height and depth, and to know Christ's love which
> surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.
>
> — Ephesians 3:18–19

*You will not get to the bottom of it. Spend your life not getting to the
bottom of it.*

---

## Day 25 — The Father himself

> …for the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me, and have
> believed that I came from God.
>
> — John 16:27

*Not tolerated by the Father because the Son intercedes. Loved by the Father
himself.*

---

## Day 26 — The same love

> Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.
>
> — John 15:9

*Measure it: the love the Father has for the Son. That is the measure.*

---

## Day 27 — Loved as he is loved

> I in them, and you in me, that they may be perfected into one; that the world
> may know that you sent me and loved them, even as you loved me.
>
> — John 17:23

*He prayed this out loud, about you, the night before he died.*

---

## Day 28 — Look at it

> Behold, how great a love the Father has given to us, that we should be called
> children of God! For this cause the world doesn't know us, because it didn't
> know him.
>
> — 1 John 3:1

*"Behold" is a command. Stop and look at it today.*

---

## Day 29 — This is what love is

> In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son
> as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.
>
> — 1 John 4:10

*The definition doesn't start with you. It never did.*

---

## Day 30 — Known and relied on

> We know and have believed the love which God has toward us. God is love, and
> he who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him. … We love him,
> because he first loved us.
>
> — 1 John 4:16, 19

*Everything you will ever give back to him is an echo of something he did
first.*

---

## When you finish

Start again at Day 1. It reads differently the second time.
