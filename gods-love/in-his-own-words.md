# In His Own Words

Passages where God speaks of his love in the first person — "I have loved you,"
"I will not forget you," "I will carry you." These are worth reading out loud.
Something in them is meant to be *heard*, not scanned.

World English Bible (public domain). **Yahweh** = **the LORD** in most Bibles.

---

## Old Testament

**Exodus 34:6–7** — God names himself
> Yahweh! Yahweh, a merciful and gracious God, slow to anger, and abundant in
> loving kindness and truth, keeping loving kindness for thousands, forgiving
> iniquity and disobedience and sin.

**Jeremiah 31:3**
> Yes, I have loved you with an everlasting love. Therefore I have drawn you
> with loving kindness.

**Isaiah 43:1**
> Don't be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by your name. You
> are mine.

**Isaiah 43:4**
> Since you have been precious and honored in my sight, and I have loved you,
> therefore I will give people in your place, and nations instead of your life.

**Isaiah 49:15–16**
> Can a woman forget her nursing child, that she should not have compassion on
> the son of her womb? Yes, these may forget, yet I will not forget you!
> Behold, I have engraved you on the palms of my hands.

**Isaiah 54:10**
> For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed, but my loving kindness
> will not depart from you, and my covenant of peace will not be removed.

**Isaiah 46:4**
> Even to old age I am he, and even to gray hairs I will carry you. I have made,
> and I will bear. Yes, I will carry, and will deliver.

**Isaiah 41:10**
> Don't you be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be dismayed, for I am your God.
> I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the
> right hand of my righteousness.

**Isaiah 62:4–5**
> You shall no more be termed "Forsaken", nor shall your land any more be termed
> "Desolate"; but you shall be called "My delight is in her" … for Yahweh
> delights in you. … As the bridegroom rejoices over the bride, so your God will
> rejoice over you.

**Jeremiah 31:20**
> Is Ephraim my dear son? Is he a darling child? For as often as I speak against
> him, I still remember him earnestly. Therefore my heart yearns for him. I will
> surely have mercy on him.

**Jeremiah 32:40–41**
> I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not turn away from
> following them, to do them good. … Yes, I will rejoice over them to do them
> good.

**Hosea 11:4**
> I drew them with cords of a man, with ties of love; and I was to them like
> those who lift up the yoke on their necks; and I bent down to him and I fed
> him.

**Hosea 14:4**
> I will heal their waywardness. I will love them freely; for my anger is turned
> away from them.

**Malachi 1:2**
> "I have loved you," says Yahweh.

## Jesus speaking

**John 15:9**
> Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.

**John 16:27**
> …for the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me, and have
> believed that I came from God.

**John 17:23** — from his prayer the night before the crucifixion
> …that the world may know that you sent me and loved them, even as you loved
> me.

**Luke 12:32**
> Don't be afraid, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give
> you the Kingdom.

**Revelation 3:19**
> As many as I love, I reprove and chasten. Be zealous therefore, and repent.

## A way to read these

Read one aloud. Then read it aloud again with your own name in it where the
text allows — "I have loved *you*, [name], with an everlasting love." Not
because the text is about you alone, but because it is not about you *less*
than everyone else. The promise was made to a people, and you are in it.
