# Twelve to Know by Heart

Short enough to memorize, load-bearing enough to be worth it. One a month gets
you all twelve in a year.

World English Bible (public domain). **Yahweh** = **the LORD** in most Bibles.

---

**1. Jeremiah 31:3**
> Yes, I have loved you with an everlasting love. Therefore I have drawn you
> with loving kindness.

**2. Isaiah 43:1**
> Don't be afraid, for I have redeemed you. I have called you by your name. You
> are mine.

**3. Isaiah 54:10**
> For the mountains may depart, and the hills be removed, but my loving kindness
> will not depart from you.

**4. Zephaniah 3:17**
> Yahweh, your God, is among you, a mighty one who will save. He will rejoice
> over you with joy. He will calm you in his love. He will rejoice over you with
> singing.

**5. Lamentations 3:22–23**
> It is because of Yahweh's loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because
> his compassion doesn't fail. They are new every morning.

**6. Psalm 103:11–12**
> For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his loving kindness
> toward those who fear him. As far as the east is from the west, so far has he
> removed our transgressions from us.

**7. Romans 5:8**
> But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners,
> Christ died for us.

**8. Romans 8:38–39**
> For I am persuaded that neither death, nor life … nor things present, nor
> things to come … nor any other created thing will be able to separate us from
> God's love which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

**9. Ephesians 2:4–5**
> But God, being rich in mercy, for his great love with which he loved us, even
> when we were dead through our trespasses, made us alive together with Christ.

**10. John 15:9**
> Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.

**11. 1 John 3:1**
> Behold, how great a love the Father has given to us, that we should be called
> children of God!

**12. 1 John 4:19**
> We love him, because he first loved us.

## A way to memorize that works

1. **Read it aloud five times**, looking at the words. Don't try to remember
   yet — just hear the shape of it.
2. **Cover the second half.** Say the first half from memory, then uncover and
   check. Then cover the whole thing.
3. **Say it once at three separate points in the day.** Spacing matters far more
   than repetition count. Three times across a day beats thirty times in ten
   minutes.
4. **Say it again the next morning, and again three days later.** That second
   recall, after you've half-forgotten it, is what moves it into long-term
   memory.
5. **Keep the reference attached.** Say "Jeremiah 31:3" before and after the
   verse every time, or you'll know the words and lose the address.

Add each new verse to a running review — say all the ones you have, in order,
once a week. Twelve verses takes about three minutes.
